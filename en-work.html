The Forum’s role

How do we work toward reconciliation?

01

A culture of peace

We promote a culture of peace, dialogue and reconciliation, and encourage young people and women to advance these values in their communities.

02

Community participation

We seek to create space for young people and women to contribute to local initiatives.

03

Cohesion and reconciliation

We support the role of culture, awareness and civic action in strengthening ties within Palestinian communities.
